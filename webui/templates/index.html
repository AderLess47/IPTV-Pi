Load from a URL

The box downloads the playlist itself, so the URL has to be reachable from your network. A saved URL is re-fetched automatically every 12 hours (keeping your group selection); uploading a file or pasting text switches that off again.

Files stay on the device. The previous playlist is kept as playlist.m3u.bak, the unfiltered one as playlist.full.m3u. This page has no login — keep it on your local network.
